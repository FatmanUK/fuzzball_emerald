Fuzzball Emerald -- player command help

Type "help <topic>" for detail on any of these.

  Getting around    look  go  home  exits
  Talking           say  pose  page  whisper
  Things            inventory  get  drop  give
  Finding out       examine  WHO  @version  @stats
  Building          @create  @dig  @open  @link  @name  @describe  @set
  Locks             @lock  @flock  @conlock  @linklock  @readlock
  Programs          @program  @edit  @list  man  mpi
  Wizards           @tune  @teleport  @toad  @boot  @force  @sanity
  Other reading     news  info  motd  @credits

Two commands are typed in capitals and mean the same thing wherever
you are: WHO lists who is connected, and QUIT disconnects you.
~
look|l|read
look                    look at the room you are in
look <thing>            look at something here, or something you carry
look <thing>=<prop>     read one property off it, if you may

Looking at a room gives its name, its description and what is in it.
It does not list the exits: a world that wants an "obvious exits"
line prints one from its own programs.
~
go|move|goto
go <exit>               leave through an exit
go home                 go to your home

You rarely need the word: typing an exit's name on its own is the
usual way to use it. "go" exists for the case where an exit shares
its name with a command.
~
home
home                    go straight to your home

Your home is where "home" sends you and where your possessions
return to if they are dropped somewhere that will not keep them. A
wizard sets it with "@link me=<room>".
~
say|"
say <message>           speak out loud
" <message>             the same, in one character

  say hello        ->  You say, "hello"
                       everyone else sees: Igor says, "hello"
~
pose|:
pose <action>           describe yourself doing something
: <action>              the same, in one character

  :waves           ->  Igor waves

Your name and a space are put in front of whatever you type, so
start with a verb.
~
page
page <player>           send them a "who is looking for you" notice
page <player>=<message> send them a message

Paging reaches someone anywhere in the world. Use whisper for
someone in the room with you.
~
whisper
whisper <player>=<message>

Speak to one person in the room. Others see that you whispered,
but not what you said.
~
inventory|i
inventory               list what you are carrying

  i                     the same, in one character
~
get|take
get <thing>             pick something up

You can only take something that is in the room with you, or in a
container here that you are allowed to reach into.
~
drop|throw
drop <thing>            put something down

Where it lands depends on the room. A room with a drop-to sends
what is dropped in it somewhere else, and a thing that is locked to
its owner goes home instead.
~
examine|ex
examine <thing>         show everything you may see about it
examine <thing>=<pattern>
                        list its properties instead

  examine here=/         the properties at the root
  examine me=**          every property, recursively

You may examine anything you control. The listing gives the flags,
the messages and locks that are set, the timestamps, the use count
and the contents.
~
@create
@create <name>[=<cost>] make a new thing, in your hands

Creating costs pennies. What you pay above the minimum is given to
the thing as its value, which is (cost-5)/5.
~
@dig
@dig <room>[=<parent>]  make a new room

Without a parent the room is placed under the environment room the
world uses by default.
~
@open|@action
@open <exit>[=<dest>]   make an exit in this room
@open <exit>;<alias>    give it more than one name

An exit with no destination is unlinked: it exists, but it goes
nowhere until someone links it.
~
@link
@link <exit>=<dest>     point an exit at somewhere
@link me=<room>         set your home
@link <thing>=<room>    set a thing's home

Linking costs pennies, and you have to be allowed to link to the
destination -- see @linklock.
~
@unlink
@unlink <exit>          make an exit go nowhere again

You must control the exit, or the room it is in.
~
@name
@name <thing>=<name>    rename something
@name me=<name> <password>
                        rename yourself

Renaming yourself needs your password, because your name is how you
log in.
~
@describe|@desc
@describe <thing>=<text>
                        set what look shows

With no text the description is removed.
~
@set
@set <thing>=<flag>     set a flag
@set <thing>=!<flag>    clear it
@set <thing>=<prop>:<value>
                        set a property
@set <thing>=<prop>:    remove a property

Flags may be abbreviated. The common ones are LINK_OK, JUMP_OK,
DARK, STICKY, HAVEN, ABODE, CHOWN_OK, BUILDER and the mucker
levels.
~
@lock|@unlock
@lock <thing>=<key>     set the lock that says who may use it
@lock <thing>           show the lock

  @flock       who may @force it
  @chlock      who may @chown it
  @conlock     who may put things in it
  @linklock    who may link to it
  @ownlock     who may pick it up
  @readlock    who may read its properties

Locks are boolean expressions over names, dbrefs and properties.
~
@find
@find [<name>]          list what you own whose name matches
~
@recycle
@recycle <thing>        destroy something and get some pennies back

What was inside it goes to its home, or to yours.
~
@password
@password <old>=<new>   change your password
~
@program|@prog
@program <name>         make a program and start editing it

You need a mucker bit. See "man" for the language itself.
~
@edit
@edit <program>         edit a program you control

Inside the editor, type "h" for its own command summary. The
argument comes first and the command last, so "3 5 d" deletes lines
three to five.
~
@list
@list <program>[=<range>]
                        show a program's source

  @list foo=10-20       just those lines
~
@version
@version                what server this is, and what it was built from
~
@stats
@stats                  how many objects of each kind the world holds
~
@tune
@tune                   list every parameter
@tune <name>            show one
@tune <name>=<value>    set one

Parameters are a runtime interface as well as a setting: MUF reads
them by name with SYSPARM, so renaming one breaks programs.
~
@teleport|@tel
@teleport <thing>=<dest>
                        move something somewhere

You need to control the thing, and the destination has to accept it.
~
@boot
@boot <player>          disconnect them
~
@toad
@toad <player>[=<heir>] turn a player into a thing

What they owned goes to the heir, or to you.
~
@force
@force <thing>=<command>
                        make something run a command as itself

Refused unless you control it and its force lock passes.
~
@ps
@ps                     list the programs currently running or waiting
~
@kill
@kill <pid>             stop one of them
~
@dump
@dump                   write everything outstanding to the database

Emerald saves continuously, so this only forces the next write
early. It does not pause the world.
~
@shutdown
@shutdown               stop the server

Everything outstanding is written first.
~
@sanity|@sanfix|@sanchange
@sanity                 check the object graph for inconsistency
@sanfix                 repair what it found
@sanchange <thing>=<field>:<value>
                        change one reference by hand

All three are God-only and cannot be abbreviated.
~
WHO
WHO                     list who is connected

Typed in capitals, and answered by the server itself, so it works
from the login screen and while a program has your input.
~
QUIT
QUIT                    disconnect

Typed in capitals, so that an ordinary word cannot disconnect you by
accident.
~
@Q
@Q                      stop the program that is reading your input

A line you type while a program is waiting goes to that program and
not to the command parser. @Q is how you get out.
~
flags
Flags are set with "@set <thing>=<flag>" and cleared with a "!" in
front of the name.

  ABODE      others may set their home here
  BUILDER    may build; "B" on a program means it is a builder tool
  CHOWN_OK   others may @chown it
  DARK       hidden from listings; on a program, traced
  HAVEN      refuses to be entered, or to be @forced
  JUMP_OK    may be teleported into
  LINK_OK    others may link to it
  QUELL      a wizard giving up wizard powers for now
  STICKY     goes home when dropped; on a program, runs as its owner
  VEHICLE    may be entered
  WIZARD     full powers
  ZOMBIE     a thing that relays what it sees to its owner

  M1 M2 M3   mucker levels: may write and run programs
~
properties|props
A property is a named value on an object. Paths look like
directories, and a leading character sets who may reach it:

  _name      readable by anyone, writable only by the owner
  @name      hidden: only a wizard sees it
  ~name      readable by anyone, set only by a wizard
  .name      private to the object's owner

Set them with "@set <thing>=<path>:<value>" and list them with
"examine <thing>=<pattern>".
~
pennies|money|cost
Building costs pennies. The prices are @tune parameters:

  object_cost   what @create costs
  room_cost     what @dig costs
  exit_cost     what @open costs
  link_cost     what @link costs

A created thing is endowed with (cost-5)/5, which is why a plain
@create leaves a thing worth one penny. Wizards pay for nothing.
