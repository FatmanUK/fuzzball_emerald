Fuzzball Emerald

A from-scratch reimplementation of Fuzzball MUCK 7 in Go, aiming for
behaviour compatibility: existing MUF programs, MPI descriptions and
.db worlds should work unchanged.

Fuzzball MUCK is the work of the Fuzzball development team and of
everyone who worked on TinyMUCK before it. Emerald owes all of its
behaviour to theirs; where the two disagree, theirs is the original.

  Fuzzball     https://www.fuzzball.org/
  Emerald      https://github.com/FatmanUK/fuzzball_emerald

Type "@version" for what this server was built from.
