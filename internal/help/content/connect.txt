Connecting

  connect <name> <password>     enter as an existing character
  create <name> <password>      make a new character
  WHO                           see who is connected
  QUIT                          disconnect
  help                          this text

If your name or password has a space in it, it cannot be typed here:
ask a wizard to change it.

Passwords are sent over an encrypted connection, but they are still
sent -- do not reuse one that matters elsewhere.
